Questions people ask first.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe what you want in everyday words. Anycreo never shows you a terminal and never asks you to make a technical decision you can't judge.

What do I need before I start?

A Windows computer and a paid plan with Claude, ChatGPT or Google's AI. Anycreo installs everything else and walks you through signing in.

What are component credits?

Components are the ready-made parts of an app: sign-in, payments, file uploads and more. You add them with credits. One credit is worth one dollar. On every paid plan, each dollar you pay comes back as a dollar of credits, so the plan pays for itself in parts you would otherwise have to build.

What can I do on the free plan?

Try Anycreo on your own idea with limited use. The free plan does not include the ready-made components. Upgrade when you want to keep building, and your payment comes back as credits.

Who can see my idea?

You and the AI company whose plan you use. Your project sits in a folder on your own computer. Anycreo does not collect your ideas or your files.

Do I need to buy a domain?

No. On a yearly Builder or Pro plan we include one domain of your choice (like yourcafe.com) at no extra cost. Already have one? Connect it instead. Monthly plans can connect a domain you already own.

Is there a Mac version?

Windows is available now. Mac is next.

What happens if I cancel?

Your app stays on your computer and keeps working there. Only the online hosting stops at the end of the period you paid for.
