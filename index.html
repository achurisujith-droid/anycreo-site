Questions people ask first.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe what you want in everyday words. Anycreo never shows you a terminal and never asks you to make a technical decision you can't judge.

What do I need before I start?

A Windows computer and a paid plan with Claude, ChatGPT or Google's AI. Anycreo installs everything else and walks you through signing in.

Why is it free to build?

The AI work runs on the plan you already pay for, so it costs us nothing to let you build. You only pay us when you want your app online for other people.

Who can see my idea?

You and the AI company whose plan you use. Your project sits in a folder on your own computer. Anycreo does not collect your ideas or your files.

Is there a Mac version?

Windows is available now. Mac is next.

What happens if I cancel?

Your app stays on your computer and keeps working there. Only the online hosting stops at the end of the period you paid for.
