Questions people ask first

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe what you want in everyday words. Anycreo never shows you code or a terminal unless you ask to see it.

What will it cost me in total?

Anycreo is free to start. You also need a paid plan with ChatGPT, Claude or Google's AI, usually about $20 a month, paid to them. Paid Anycreo plans start at $10 a month and come back to you as credits for ready-made parts.

What are credits?

Credits pay for ready-made parts like sign-in or card payments. One credit is one dollar. Whatever you pay for your plan comes back as the same amount in credits, and the parts you add stay in your app.

What can I do on the free plan?

Try Anycreo on your own idea with limited use and see it running on your PC. Ready-made parts like sign-in and payments come with paid plans.

Do I need to buy a domain?

No. On a yearly Builder or Pro plan we include one domain of your choice, like yourcafe.com. Already have one? Connect it instead.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is that normal?

Yes, for new apps. Windows warns about programs it hasn't seen many people download yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Anycreo installs only into your user folder and can be removed from Settings like any app.

Is there a Mac version?

Windows is available now. Mac is next. Email hello@anycreo.com and we'll tell you when it's ready.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you change your mind, ask within 14 days of paying and we'll refund you in full, as long as you haven't used any of your credits yet.

Why is the first year cheaper?

Most people need a few months to finish their first app and get it in front of customers. The first-year price gives you that time at a lower cost. After the first year, plans renew at the standard yearly price, and we'll remind you before it happens.

What happens if I cancel?

Your app stays on your PC and keeps working there. Online hosting stops at the end of the period you paid for, and you can download your data first.
